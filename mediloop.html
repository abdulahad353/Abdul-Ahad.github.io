01 · The problem

Medication management is a connected journey.

The proposal frames medication adherence as a challenge involving patients, caregivers and pharmacies. A dispenser alone would not solve the whole problem: reminders, visibility and refill continuity also matter.

  • Map user needs across the medication journey
  • Identify friction points between dispensing and refilling
  • Consider trust, usability and operational feasibility
02 · The solution concept

Connect the steps around the user.

The concept combines a smart dispenser, app reminders and tracking, caregiver alerts, and pharmacy refill or delivery connections.

  • Schedule → Fill → Dispense
  • Remind → Alert → Refill
  • Consider rental and purchase options
03 · What I worked on

Link product logic to business logic.

I worked across the business-plan structure, connecting the ecosystem and operating model with market opportunity, commercial options, unit economics, financial projections, risks and the funding narrative.

  • Business model and pricing scenarios
  • Market sizing and commercial assumptions
  • Funding allocation and execution priorities
04 · What I learned

A product is only one part of the system.

Healthcare concepts must work for users and caregivers while accounting for pharmacies, operations, economics, compliance and trust. The business system around the product matters as much as the product proposition.

Important context
