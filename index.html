Case study · Mediloop

More than a device: a connected-care business model.

The supplied project material positions Mediloop around medication adherence, caregiver visibility, connected technology and circular healthcare.

Problem → solution

Medication management was treated as a system problem.

The concept brings together a smart dispenser, mobile app, caregiver visibility, pharmacy integration and cloud/AI capabilities. The operating flow is structured around Schedule → Fill → Dispense → Remind → Alert → Refill.

Business model

Three commercial routes.

  • Rental: €29/month
  • Purchase: €249
  • Purchase + subscription: €249 + €19/month
Unit economics

Designed around a tangible hardware margin.

  • Selling price: €249
  • Landed cost: €155
  • Gross profit: €94
  • Gross margin: 37.8%
Market + finance

From market opportunity to funding logic.

  • TAM: €28B+
  • SAM: €3.6B+
  • SOM: €17.4M
  • Funding ask: €600,000
Financial direction

A multi-year commercial model.

  • Year 1 revenue: €498K
  • Year 2: €1.494M
  • Year 3: €2.988M
  • Year 4: €4.482M
  • Year 5: €6.225M
  • Positive EBITDA from Year 2 in the supplied deck.
What I worked across

Strategy · research · finance · product · operations · sustainability.

The project required connecting product vision with market research, customer problems, business-model design, financial projections, go-to-market thinking, stakeholder ecosystem, operational logic, risk and circularity. The sustainability concept included reusable trays, repairable/modular design, take-back, refurbishment/remanufacturing and rental-based reuse.

Case study · Fantitos

An ongoing venture, not a finished assignment.

The work continues as an exploration of how AI and personalisation can improve social gaming.

Customer discovery

Start with what users actually experience.

The project included 20 semi-structured interviews with university students and young professionals in Spain and India. Recurring problems included repetitive gameplay, lack of personalisation, awkward content, complex setup and limited variety.

Product question

Can AI make the experience more personal?

The concept explores AI-powered social and party gaming, using personalisation and content generation as potential differentiators.

Commercial lens

Product + market + economics.

The work considers market sizing, MAU scenarios, freemium monetisation, subscriptions and premium content packs rather than treating product design in isolation.

Current status

Keep testing the assumptions.

Fantitos is presented as an ongoing venture exploration. The purpose is to keep learning about users, product-market fit, differentiation and growth rather than claiming that every assumption is already validated.

Case study · Going International

European Commission project: selecting SMEs with international potential.

The supplied project document is a detailed 107-page internationalisation programme for Argentinean SMEs.

Project objective

Identify, select and promote Argentinean SMEs with high growth potential.

The project builds a structured funnel from an initial pool of SMEs toward a smaller cohort, then moves into strategic and organisational diagnosis, international benchmarking and market positioning, and finally an international development roadmap.

Phase 1

Screening & selection.

  • Questionnaire-based assessment
  • Scorecard and selection logic
  • Strategic motivation & readiness
  • Operational, financial and organisational capability
  • Top-management commitment & readiness
Phase 2.1

Strategic & organisational diagnosis.

  • Business model & value proposition
  • Organisational structure & governance
  • Financial & operational capabilities
  • Strategic orientation & internal alignment
  • Diagnostic workshops with management
Phase 2.2

International benchmarking & positioning.

  • Market attractiveness & entry feasibility
  • International competitive benchmarking
  • Value proposition, pricing, quality and innovation
  • Porter 5 Forces
  • Differentiation and strategic trade-offs
Phase 2.3

Three-year international development roadmap.

  • International strategic objectives
  • Market prioritisation & entry logic
  • Organisational capability development
  • Operational/process adjustments
  • Phasing, prioritisation and governance
Analytical toolkit

PESTEL · SWOT · VRIO · Porter 5 Forces · Market analysis · Benchmarking · Financial & operational assessment.

The document also includes Argentina market analysis across Buenos Aires, Rosario, Córdoba and Mendoza, stakeholder mapping, risk mitigation, communication planning, Gantt charts and project budgeting.
